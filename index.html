Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Fin UniQ is a personal finance management application designed to help users manage income, expenses, loans, investments and financial records.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fin UniQ handles information when you use the application.

1. Information Stored in the App

Fin UniQ allows users to enter financial information such as income, expenses, loan details, investments, transactions and reminders.

This information is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage. The app is designed to operate offline and does not require an account or registration.

2. Information Collection and Sharing

Fin UniQ does not operate its own cloud service for collecting or storing your financial records. The app does not transmit your financial records to our servers.

We do not sell or share your financial records with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Backups and Exported Files

The app may allow you to export a backup of your financial information to a file on your device or to a location you choose.

Backup files may contain sensitive financial information. You are responsible for keeping these files secure and for deciding whether to share them with anyone.

4. Biometric Authentication

Fin UniQ may offer device-supported biometric authentication to help protect access to the app.

Biometric verification is handled by your Android device and its operating system. Fin UniQ does not access or store your fingerprint or facial biometric data.

5. Notifications

Fin UniQ may schedule local notifications for loan EMI dates, payment reminders and other app reminders.

These notifications are managed on your device. You can control notification permissions through Android settings.

6. Data Security

Financial information is stored on your device. You should protect your device with a secure screen lock and avoid sharing exported backup files with untrusted people.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information stored on your device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your financial records remain stored on your device until you delete them or remove the app's stored data.

You can delete app data through Android settings or uninstall the application. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved them.

8. Children's Privacy

Fin UniQ is a general personal finance management application and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
